Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the What You See Android app (published by an independent developer) collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. The app does not require an account and does not show ads.

Information We Collect

The app includes a small analytics module that collects:

  • A random identifier generated on your device on first launch. It is not tied to your name, account, or advertising ID, and it is not your device's hardware ID.
  • Your device model and operating system version.
  • General gameplay progress statistics, such as how many sessions and days you've played, in-game currency earned, settings you've toggled, and which constellations from the in-game catalog you've unlocked.
  • Basic crash and error reports (message, file, and line number), if the game encounters an unexpected error.

How Information Is Used

This information is used only to understand how the app is used in aggregate and to find and fix bugs. It is not used for advertising, and it is not sold to anyone.

Third-Party Service Providers

Analytics data is processed by PostHog, hosted in the EU region, under their own privacy policy. No other third-party service receives data from the app.

What Is Never Collected

We do not collect anything you type or create in the app — no names you give your constellations, no other custom text, and no precise location. There are no accounts, no ads, and no third-party ad or tracking networks.

Data Retention and Your Choices

Analytics data is retained by our processor under its own data-protection terms. Because the device identifier is anonymous and generated locally, we have no way to look up or delete data for a specific person without more information from you.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this same page with a new "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to nezzzetic@gmail.com.
