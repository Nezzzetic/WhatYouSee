Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

What You See is a calm, meditative star-constellation puzzle. This page explains what data the game collects, where it goes, and why.

Web version collects nothing

The browser version of the game — played on GitHub Pages or on itch.io — makes zero external network requests. It has no analytics, no trackers, no cookies, and sends nothing anywhere. Everything about your play session stays in your browser's local storage, on your device, and is never transmitted.

Android app: what is collected

The Android app build includes a minimal analytics module that helps us understand how the game is used and fix bugs. It is not present in the web version.

  • Processor: PostHog Cloud, hosted in the EU region (eu.i.posthog.com).
  • Identifier: a random string the app generates for itself on first launch, stored only on your device. It is not your account, not your device ID, and not an advertising ID — we have no way to tell who you are.
  • Gameplay snapshot: every event carries a fixed set of 21 small fields — plain numbers and short strings describing overall progress (session counts, days played, stars collected, in‑game currency earned, settings toggles, and similar totals). The only non‑numeric field is your device model and Android version (not your full device fingerprint or user agent string). The exact field list is:

  • Crash reports: if the game hits an unexpected error, we record the error message, file, line number and stack trace, capped at three reports per session.

What is never collected

We do not collect anything you type or create in the game: no constellation names, no canvas titles, no coordinates, and no other content from your save file. There are no ads and no third‑party ad networks in the game.

Data retention and requests

Analytics events are stored by our processor, PostHog (EU), under their own data‑protection terms. Since the identifier is anonymous and generated on your device, we cannot look up or delete data tied to a specific person without additional information from you. If you have a question about this policy or want to reach us about your data, write to nezzzetic@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this same page with a new "Last updated" date above.
